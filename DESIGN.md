# Syntax Studio — In relation

Current direction contract, 7 September 2026. This supersedes Common Ground and the literal Open House composition. The latest user brief retains the new Syntax identity, warm neutral, deep brown, pale blue, premium tone and three-practice architecture. It asks for substantially different structures inside that identity and delegates selection of the strongest. The selected direction is A, **In relation**, evaluated in [the structural exploration](docs/rebrand/structural-exploration-2026-09.md) and shown alongside three alternatives in [the real-media prototypes](docs/rebrand/structures.html). This records that design decision; it does not imply a separate user approval or completed implementation QA.

## The governing idea

Syntax is the relationship between specialist contributions. ISO400 owns Image & Design; 35mm owns Film & VFX; Nyfane owns Technology. Each can lead independently and collaborate with the others. Syntax supplies the creative framework connecting them, rather than acting as a fourth practice.

Make that relationship visible through position, proportion, overlap, crop and sequence. Three real media artifacts share a field, and the visitor can change what leads while retaining the others as context. The page's recognizable quality comes from this compositional rule and its deliberate recurrence, not from equal practice panels or a new decorative motif.

## Identity and color

Use the supplied Syntax wordmark, full lockup and symbol as artwork. Preserve their original contours, spacing and aspect ratios. Scale the wordmark uniformly; never stretch, skew, redraw, split or crop its letters to force a layout. The opening gives the whole parent mark architectural scale. Smaller practice names enter through media attribution before they become prominent navigation or editorial content.

Paper `#e9e7e2` is the shared environment; ink `#31291f` carries text. Timber `#382b23` creates a cinematic interval and contact depth. Sky `#b5cddd` belongs to a shared response field or a quiet typographic passage. Dark blue `#24485b` remains a supporting interaction color. Colors can cross practice boundaries; none permanently identifies a practice. Check contrast in every pairing, including captions over media.

Keep the original colors inside photographs, artwork and website captures. ISO400's blackletter, print texture and serif world stay within its attributed work. Nyfane's plum/coral tabs remain inside its website artifact, not in the Syntax shell. Use the supplied 35mm Productions SVG at `/brand/35mm.svg` unchanged, with its white lettering and distressed texture on a brown background.

On mobile: the same clear parent ownership with a separately composed vertical media field. The opening must show the mark and meaningful work together; do not spend the screen on a slogan or explanation before revealing media.

The default composition gives the ISO400 portrait the strongest photographic presence. A campaign film poster crosses it at a different proportion, and a smaller Nyfane website capture provides a digital artifact. These are three items in one field, never three enclosing product or service panels. Restrict the opening to these three artifacts and one control group. Preserve recognizable subjects, visible attribution and direct access to work.

Use a short lead-in such as “Change the relationship” to explain the interaction. At common mobile widths, keep the complete mark, real media and controls close to the first screen; allow vertical breathing space rather than shrinking labels to meet a fixed pixel target. Core content is present before interaction and before JavaScript enhancement.

## Type, grid and rhythm

DM Sans remains the text family through `--font-house`; the supplied logo supplies its own letterforms. Build hierarchy through scale, weight, line breaks and offset alignment. Display text is light in density, generally weight 400 with tight tracking and open enough line-height to preserve the forms. Reading text stays around 16–18px with a 1.6 line-height and a useful measure of roughly 45–65 characters. Captions are quieter, but status, credits and controls must remain readable.

Keep the existing fluid gutter, `clamp(20px, 4.4vw, 76px)`, and maximum content width of 1720px. Use shared axes and uneven spans rather than equal columns. A displaced edge must relate to another edge, caption or subject; asymmetry is composed, not random. Keep media rectangular and framing spare. Shadows, tilted mounted prints, permanent room backgrounds and literal door geometry are not the parent system.

The page sequence is identity → real-work composition → concise point of view → specialist relationships → campaign proof → wider work → founders → contact. Alternate close image detail, broad empty space, dark screening, open practice rows and compact factual captions. Do not repeat the hero collage or give every passage a full viewport. Keep ordinary Work, Practices, Studio and Contact routes clear; detailed services belong chiefly to the specialist sites.

## Signature interaction

A native labeled range and discrete preset buttons change the same shared media coordinates. The range permits continuous adjustment; presets provide direct states without requiring drag. Changing the leading medium retains context from the other two. It changes visual hierarchy, not project attribution or the studio's organizational model.

Preset changes use one 650ms `cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)` transition. Direct manipulation follows input immediately so the media does not lag behind the pointer. Reduced motion applies each chosen composition instantly; it does not remove the interaction or its meaning. Keep a useful static default if JavaScript is unavailable. There is no entrance loader, idle recomposition, scroll trap, required horizontal scroll, automatic word reveal or decorative custom cursor.

Use actual button and range semantics, a visible label, an understandable value description, clear preset selected state, and visible keyboard focus. Preserve native range arrow/Home/End operation and ordinary button activation. Announce meaningful settled state changes without flooding assistive technology on every input tick. All essential captions and destinations are available without hover. Interactive targets should provide at least a comfortable 44px touch area, even when their visible type is small.

## Media and truth

Current usable evidence is specific:

- **ISO400 Street portraits:** real supplied photography, presented as an image study. Client, year and subject identity remain unspecified unless source evidence establishes them.
- **Anonymized burger campaign:** actual Syntax stills, finished artwork and films. Preserve existing anonymity and historical Syntax Studio production credit. A present-day discipline relationship does not establish an individual founder's historical authorship.
- **Nyfane website study:** an actual capture of its own implemented website, explicitly marked as a studio study in development. It is not commissioned client work or evidence of a public launch. Its fictional demo projects must not enter the Syntax portfolio.

The opening juxtaposes separate work artifacts as a studio composition; it must not imply that they are a single all-practice commission. Shared project attribution is allowed only when verified. Real founder names and practice mapping come from the existing content/provenance. No invented clients, outcomes, awards, testimonials, statistics, film stills or credentials.

Optimize the portrait and capture derivatives; never deliver the large original photographic sources to the opening. Use responsive sources and explicit geometry to avoid layout shift. Initial film is a poster, with zero video payload required for first paint. Insert/load film on explicit play intent, retain native controls, pause when appropriate, and provide a useful fallback. Preserve native film ratio during playback with contain framing; a poster crop is not permission to crop the film itself. Each case can use image pairs, portrait sequences, film and interface captures according to its medium.

## Mobile and accessibility

Mobile is a vertical relationship: portrait leads, film crosses a lower edge, and the website capture sits at an offset upper or middle position. Define mobile coordinates deliberately; never scale down the desktop arrangement wholesale. Keep the full mark, the portrait's focal subject, meaningful film poster detail and readable labels. Range and presets remain reachable, and every preset must fit the viewport without horizontal overflow.

Support both Norwegian and English copy without forced identical line lengths. Use semantic headings, useful alternative text, native links and controls, a skip link, visible focus, sufficient contrast, and stable focus when states change. Captions and provenance remain legible in every arrangement. The menu exposes its expanded state and is usable without animation. Forms use explicit labels, adjacent error/help text and a direct contact fallback. Videos requiring captions must have them before being treated as complete accessible content.

## Completion standard

Treat this document as the design contract, not a claim that all rules already pass. Validate the implemented homepage, work index, representative project, studio, contact and shared navigation at desktop and mobile widths. Review the opening and each preset for parent dominance, crop, credit readability and practical contact access; test range/button keyboard behavior, reduced motion and film loading. Complete appropriate build, accessibility, SEO and performance checks separately. Record material limitations honestly.

The final question is whether the page demonstrates disciplines interacting through one Syntax while remaining commercially clear. A polished gallery with a changeable collage is insufficient if the rest of the page loses that relationship.
