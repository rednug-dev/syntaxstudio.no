# Syntax: four structures, one identity

7 September 2026. Read alongside the ecosystem audit, current reference study and technical baseline in this directory. This continues the approved warm-paper / brown / pale-blue identity. It does not reopen the logo, practice architecture or typography direction.

The brief explicitly asks us to evaluate and select the strongest structure. Selection below is that delegated decision, not a claim of additional user approval. The Impeccable Windows context launcher failed to execute; the written skill guidance and the user's staged workflow were followed manually. These are code-native structural studies using actual assets, not generated project imagery.

## What the audit changes

ISO400 owns the image as an authored object: considered crops, portraits, print, photographic sequence. Its decorative wordmark and its print devices remain inside its own practice. Nyfane owns usable digital systems and a strongly particular plum/coral, tab-based interface. Its engineering standards transfer; its tabs, palette and fictional demo clients do not. The real Nyfane website can appear as a labeled studio study. 35mm owns time, film and VFX; existing Syntax campaign films supply actual moving-image evidence, with historical Syntax production credits retained. Syntax owns relationships between these distinct media and people.

The opportunity is a composition with shared coordinates but different proportions. It lets a visitor change what leads without losing what connects. This is a strategic opening observed in the reference set, not a claim that no other studio has ever used overlapping media.

## A — In relation (selected)

1. **Core idea:** the visitor composes the relationship between three real media artifacts. One foreground changes; the other disciplines stay present.
2. **Why Syntax:** separate visual elements acquire meaning through position, proportion and sequence. This is the studio's operating model made tangible.
3. **First five seconds:** the supplied Syntax mark occupies the width of the page. Real ISO400 photography enters immediately beneath it; a smaller film and website capture intersect its field.
4. **First viewport:** quiet navigation, large unmodified identity, short Oslo/independent-house statement, then one asymmetric composition. No three enclosing practice panels.
5. **Flow:** identity → live work composition → concise creative point of view → specialist relationships → campaign proof → wider work stream → founders → contact.
6. **Real work:** Street portraits from ISO400, existing anonymised Syntax campaign film, and Nyfane's actual website development capture. Their different provenance is explicit.
7. **ISO400 introduction:** a dominant portrait and an image-study caption before the practice name becomes a large typographic selector.
8. **35mm introduction:** an overlapping poster becomes a playable film on demand. Expanding motion gives the film its original portrait ratio.
9. **Nyfane introduction:** the composition itself responds. The actual Nyfane website is a separately labeled studio study, not invented commissioned work.
10. **Interaction between practices:** a native range and discrete buttons interpolate shared frame coordinates. All three media remain part of one field.
11. **Syntax dominance:** the mark and master composition are larger than any practice label. Practice art lives within individual artifacts, never as the page's default visual language.
12. **Typography:** existing DM Sans; mark at architectural scale; one expressive statement with offset line alignment; small readable captions; large open practice rows.
13. **Motion:** one structural moment. Interpolate position, size and crop; use a single 650ms exponential ease for preset changes, direct response while dragging, instant reduced-motion states. No entrance loader or repeating marquees.
14. **Colour:** paper is the shared environment; brown supports the campaign screening; pale blue appears in the response field and later in a quiet statement. No permanent practice-colour mapping.
15. **Signature:** “Change the relationship.” The same real media recompose continuously from stillness to motion to interaction, with keyboard-equivalent controls.
16. **Mobile:** a deliberately vertical, overlapping field; portrait leads, film crosses its lower edge, digital capture occupies an offset upper corner. The full mark, work and controls fit close to the first screen. No sideways scroll or desktop scaled down.
17. **Performance:** two small initial posters plus responsive portrait; zero initial video; no canvas, no 3D engine, no motion dependency. Fixed media geometry prevents layout shift.
18. **Premium:** the work carries detail and texture while the page supplies exact relationships and generous pauses.
19. **Memorable:** the visitor changes the hierarchy and sees the studio's idea happen.
20. **Retained:** supplied Syntax mark, DM Sans, current warm paper, deep brown, pale blue, premium restraint, three founder-led practices.
21. **Risk:** overlapping surfaces can become a collage. Limit the opening to three artifacts, one control, fixed edge relationships and clear captions. The quiet lower page must not repeat the same trick.

## B — The shared sentence

1. **Core / Syntax:** a large brand statement is interrupted by actual work, with media behaving as punctuation between ideas.
2. **First five seconds / viewport:** full-width Syntax mark, then a compact editorial statement split by a wide cropped campaign photograph. Work begins in the first viewport.
3. **Flow:** identity → campaign photograph → thesis → image/motion/technology typographic sentence → project stream → founder notes → contact.
4. **Real work / ISO400:** portrait pairs interrupt the reading column, keeping native image ratios and outside captions.
5. **35mm:** a dark full-width pause opens a deliberate screening after a short still sequence.
6. **Nyfane:** a website capture enters the text flow; a responsive crop demonstrates interaction without imitating Nyfane's navigation.
7. **Relationship:** the same alignment crosses text, still and film; project attributions expose where disciplines overlap.
8. **Syntax dominance / type:** the logo and parent voice lead; text is architecture, with 96px desktop display and compact body measures.
9. **Motion:** a photograph widens into the following screening through a shared crop. Native page transitions elsewhere.
10. **Colour:** paper throughout; a single brown film interval and a pale-blue closing thought.
11. **Signature:** a held line of type opens to admit project media, then resolves back into readable copy.
12. **Mobile:** text and image form short alternating beats; no pinned sequence. Work is visible below the first two-line statement.
13. **Performance:** almost entirely server-rendered, one small intersection observer, intent-loaded film.
14. **Premium / memorable:** exceptional editorial rhythm. Risk: without deeper motion it is still recognisable as a high-end portfolio convention.
15. **Retains:** all identity commitments listed in A.

## C — From frame to frame

1. **Core / Syntax:** one campaign evolves from photograph to film to digital presentation; continuity explains collaboration.
2. **First five seconds / viewport:** supplied identity over a brown screening field, one enormous still and a visible sequence control. Film does not load automatically.
3. **Flow:** still → film → responsive composition → practice credits → other work → people → contact.
4. **Real work / ISO400:** the burger-build still opens; its exact composition is compared with the existing burger-build film.
5. **35mm:** the matched film starts on explicit play and occupies the central frame without destructive cropping.
6. **Nyfane:** the visitor adjusts the layout around the two real assets. This is a Syntax website experiment, not a claimed historical digital campaign deliverable.
7. **Relationship:** continuity between modes makes disciplines dependencies, not service categories.
8. **Syntax dominance / type:** large fixed identity boundary and restrained film-like captions. No fabricated timestamps or production metadata.
9. **Motion:** view-driven frame geometry with native scrolling and a complete static fallback; no multi-screen scroll trap.
10. **Colour:** cinematic brown lead, then paper; pale blue as a temporary interaction state.
11. **Signature:** a match-cut between the supplied still and film, then user control over their relationship.
12. **Mobile:** three short vertical beats with direct next/play controls; film in its native portrait orientation.
13. **Performance:** best with additional matched media; current footage must remain click-to-load and cannot masquerade as a broad showreel.
14. **Premium / memorable:** strong cinema and continuity. Risk: one food campaign could narrow perception of the entire house, and Nyfane's contribution is less immediately evidenced.
15. **Retains:** all identity commitments listed in A.

## D — A variable index

1. **Core / Syntax:** the homepage is a curated, changeable index whose scale follows the discipline being explored.
2. **First five seconds / viewport:** full Syntax wordmark shares the screen with a dominant named project, smaller offset previews and an always-visible work list.
3. **Flow:** work index → selected relationship → practice explanation → founders → enquiry.
4. **Real work / ISO400:** portrait and campaign imagery lead the default index with per-image cropping.
5. **35mm:** selecting film gives one screening frame the available space, while other titles remain legible.
6. **Nyfane:** selecting the technology study brings the actual website capture forward; no fake client list.
7. **Relationship:** projects can appear under several practices. The same index reorganises without losing the user’s position.
8. **Syntax dominance / type:** logo as permanent top boundary; projects as typographic anchors, compact explanatory prose.
9. **Motion:** reorder/resize with stable DOM order. Selected-item scale changes instead of every image animating in.
10. **Colour:** neutral index, brown active media, pale blue as temporary selected field.
11. **Signature:** a project expands from its indexed position into an editorial detail view while its neighbours become related work.
12. **Mobile:** one featured item followed by a readable list with touch selection. No miniature desktop grid.
13. **Performance:** little initial media; limit prefetching and avoid loading every project on each filter change.
14. **Premium / memorable:** clarity and curation. Risk: needs a larger approved project library to reach its strongest expression and could feel like ISO400's project index.
15. **Retains:** all identity commitments listed in A.

**Select A.** It makes the company model experiential, establishes the parent immediately and supports the actual media available now. Borrow B's disciplined pacing as a standard, not its layout. C is too dependent on one campaign and D needs more approved work. This is a structural decision within the same identity, not selection of the loudest visual concept.
