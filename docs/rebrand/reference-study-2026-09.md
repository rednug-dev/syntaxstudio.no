# Syntax — contemporary reference study

Reviewed 7 September 2026 (Europe/Oslo). This study informs structure inside the existing Syntax identity; it is not a proposal to borrow another studio's visual language.

## Evidence and limits

All 13 official homepages below were opened in the web research tool and inspected in a live Chromium browser at 1440 × 1000 and 390 × 844. Screens were observed after initial navigation and, where loaders remained, revisited after settling. “Opening” means observed first impression, not a controlled five-second usability or performance test. These are desktop viewport and narrow-viewport observations, not physical-device testing. Scroll was sampled on Feelm and Active Theory; ManvsMachine's cookie rejection was exercised. Other menus, filters, full films, project transitions, keyboard access, reduced motion and touch gestures were not exhaustively tested. Motion claims below distinguish observed changes from controls or editorial intent. No site is being certified for accessibility, performance or conversion.

The live sites differ materially from older inspiration screenshots: Koto now resolves to koto.com, DIA to dia.studio, PORTO ROCHA uses a project sidebar, and COLLINS leads with its premium/value proposition. Old Active Theory v5 and a ManvsMachine staging mirror appeared in search; they were excluded as representations of the current homepage.

## What Syntax can credibly own

This is a directional inference from a sample, not proof that no studio has ever done it. The reviewed openings predominantly emphasize a proposition, a reel, an archive or an immersive technical world. The less-developed opportunity in this sample is **the relationship between specialist contributions made visible inside the work itself**.

Syntax can own composition as a working relationship. A photograph, a moving sequence and a digital surface should share a frame, affect one another, then resolve into a coherent project encounter. One practice's output becomes another's input. The parent identity is the enduring rule that governs this exchange. That fits the meaning of Syntax and can remain recognizable without permanently color-coding three entities.

The mechanism must be honest. Do not imply that unrelated existing projects were made collaboratively. Distinct references can form a clearly labelled studio composition; only verified shared credits may be presented as a collaborative case. Do not invent a 35mm film to fill a visual slot.

### Principles for the structural exploration

1. **Identity and work arrive together.** Use the current Syntax mark as the primary architectural scale, with a real image or film fragment in the same initial composition. Aim for useful work visibility within the first mobile viewport, with no compulsory loader.
2. **A relationship persists through the page.** Carry one seam, alignment, framing rule or sequence from the opening into project presentation and practice attribution. Avoid unrelated section-by-section effects.
3. **Use one structural transformation.** A media boundary may open, recompose or transfer its image to the next context. The resulting state should also make sense as a static composition. Motion explains relationships; it does not hide content until a scroll milestone.
4. **Reveal the specialists after proof.** Let a media caption introduce ISO400, 35mm or Nyfane in context, then clarify independent practice and collaboration. Avoid a first-screen company selector.
5. **Compose mobile separately.** Preserve a focal crop and a trace of the next medium, shorten the opening, and replace hover dependency with explicit tap/keyboard controls. Do not squeeze a desktop triptych into the phone.
6. **Alternate density and silence.** A dense opening relationship, an unhurried full-width image, a concise idea, an active practice moment, a compact work sequence, and direct human contact give rhythm without ten full-height sections.
7. **Borrow standards, not signatures.** Instrument's hierarchy, Feelm's conviction, Pentagram's attribution, BUCK's curation, and Resn's explicit exit are principles. Their typography, layouts, colors, loaders, graphics and effects stay theirs.
8. **Trust stays factual and local to the work.** Real project title, client when verified, practice, discipline and a short specific context do more for this stage of Syntax than a synthetic logo wall or unsupported scale claims.
9. **The experimental layer earns its place.** Work, Studio and Contact remain plain routes. Static posters, readable headings, reduced-motion states, keyboard access and ordinary scrolling are the baseline design.

### Structural hypotheses worth testing within the same identity

- **A shared seam:** the Syntax wordmark and two real-media fields use a common alignment; movement recomposes their relationship before settling into work.
- **An editorial sequence:** parent-scale typography interrupted by still, film and interface material, with a repeatable handoff between media and credited practice.
- **A working composition:** one curated interactive media arrangement with a simple way to change relationships, followed by a calm project stream.
- **A cinematic cut:** a short opening composition resolves directly into a flagship project; practice attribution is revealed as part of the edit rather than three parallel panels.

These are structural hypotheses, not separate brand concepts. Evaluate them with the ecosystem audit, available assets, mobile composition and performance budget before choosing.
